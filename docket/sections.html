Notes

  • Gaps in arbot that decided outcomes.
    • There is no source of public security flags or labels (§ 2.7). No trusted, free provider is known.
    • It can't make a view call to, or read the storage of, an arbitrary contract, such as a router's owner or facets.
    • It can't give the time of a past block.
    • It can't decode an argument that is a tuple, such as a swap's beneficiary, or show a simulation's logs, so it can't say who a swap pays.
    The last three are candidates for the add-evidence skill.
